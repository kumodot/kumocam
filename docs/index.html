Your camera footage,
organized in one click.

KumoCam is the import companion built for the DJI Osmo Pocket 4/4P - and any camera. Organize into date folders with smart metadata names, convert D-Log with LUTs, stitch panoramas and geotag. Your original files are never touched.

⬇ Download for Windows ☕ Support on Ko-fi View on GitHub

Simple installer or portable .zip · FFmpeg included · macOS: run from source

See it in action

Scan, review, import, convert with LUTs, merge - a real session, start to finish. Originals are never touched.

Everything between camera and edit

The boring parts of the footage pipeline, handled.

Lightroom-style import

Date folders, PHOTOS / VIDEOS / PANORAMA split, and filenames enriched with resolution, fps, gamma and slow-motion tags - file dates untouched.

Convert with LUTs

Batch 4K to 1080p with any .cube LUT (D-Log to Rec.709 and beyond), merge clips, mute audio per clip - FFmpeg ships inside the app.

Panorama stitching

Stitch the Osmo's 3x3 grid and 180° panoramas right on your PC - with honest advice when the official app would do a better job.

Geotagging

Cameras without GPS? Pick a point on a map in your browser, copy from a reference photo, or paste coordinates - applied to whole batches.

Camera profiles

Auto-detects your camera from metadata (Osmo Pocket, Lumix, iPhone out of the box) - and any camera can be added with a small JSON file.

Safe by design

Originals are only ever read. Duplicate detection warns before re-importing, corrupted-looking files are flagged, and drives eject safely from the app.
